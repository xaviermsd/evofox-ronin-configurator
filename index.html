1. Check the power switch

The ON/OFF switch is on the right edge of the keyboard, next to the → key. Set it to ON for 2.4 GHz, or plug the Type-C cable in firmly for wired mode.

2. Wake Up Sleeping RF Link

If inactive for a few minutes, the Ronin switches to deep sleep to conserve battery. Tap the Spacebar once to wake the 2.4GHz link before clicking Connect.

3. Direct Motherboard USB Port

If using a USB hub or monitor pass-through, plug the 2.4GHz receiver or USB-C cable directly into a motherboard USB 2.0 or 3.0 port on your PC.

4. Browser Permissions Dialog

Ensure you select Unknown Device (1A2C:A157) or 2.4 Wireless Device in the browser prompt and click Connect (avoid generic standard keyboard entry if shown).

WebHID Setup & Troubleshooting Guide

1

Select Keyboard Mode

Press FN + 4 for 2.4 GHz wireless or FN + 5 for Type-C wired. Verify by holding Fn to see key 4 or 5 light up.

2

Plug in Receiver or Cable

Insert the 2.4 GHz USB receiver into a USB port on your PC, or plug the braided Type-C cable firmly into the keyboard.

3

Authorize WebHID

Click Connect Device in this app and select the recognized device: Unknown Device (1A2C:A157) or 2.4 Wireless Device.

Why can't I configure over Bluetooth (Fn+1/2/3)?

Over Bluetooth, your computer doesn’t let browsers reach the keyboard’s settings channel (0xFF02:0x0002), so this app can’t see it. Bluetooth is great for everyday typing; switch to 2.4G (Fn+4) or Wired USB (Fn+5) to tune lighting or remap keys.

The 2.4 GHz receiver isn’t recognised

Long press FN + 4 until the LED blinks fast, with the receiver plugged in. That re-pairs the keyboard with it.

How do I perform a complete factory reset?

Press and hold FN + Esc for 5 seconds. This resets all settings to default. You can also use Restore Factory Defaults in Device Settings.

How does battery charging work?

Charge it with the provided Type-C cable. In Bluetooth mode, the battery percentage shows on the connected device. In 2.4 GHz or wired mode, this app shows it in the top bar.
